# AMOS Desktop 1.0.55

The account menu now uses one Company & account dropdown. It includes the current login’s organizations and other saved accounts’ last-used companies. Choosing another account refreshes the same dropdown with that login’s organizations. Saved accounts with matching company and email labels remain distinguishable.

The menu keeps its right-side padding with multiple companies, long names, and smaller windows. Controls and text fit within the menu instead of forcing horizontal scrolling. A failed switch restores the current selection, and the selector stays disabled while a switch is in progress.

Browser validation covers single and multiple accounts, long labels, smaller windows, both switching actions, failed switches, and offline reconnect choices. The menu and document remain within their available widths across the tested viewport sizes.
